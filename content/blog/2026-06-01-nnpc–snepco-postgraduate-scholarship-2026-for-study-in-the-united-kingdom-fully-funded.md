Applications are now open for the **2026 NNPC–SNEPCo Postgraduate Scholarship. Shell Nigeria Exploration and Production Company Limited (SNEPCo)**, in partnership with NNPC Limited and co-venture partners, invites applications for the 2026 NNPC–SNEPCo Postgraduate Scholarship.



The scholarship supports one-year Master’s degree programmes at the University of Aberdeen and Newcastle University in the United Kingdom.



**Participating Universities & Courses**

**University of Aberdeen**

1. MSc Geophysics
2. MSc Safety & Reliability Engineering for Oil & Gas
3. MSc Advanced Chemical Engineering
4. MSc Integrated Petroleum Geoscience
5. Newcastle University
6. MSc Data Science and AI
7. MSc Data Science
8. MSc Engineering Management
9. MSc Sustainable Chemical Engineering
10. MSc Artificial Intelligence
11. MSc Robotics and AI
12. MSc Marine and Ocean Engineering





**Eligibility Criteria**

Applicants must:

* Display intellectual ability and leadership potential
* Have an adequate standard of English (at least 6.5 in IELTS) and meet the university’s language requirements
* Have been offered admission to study one of the eligible courses
* Hold (or expect to hold before the scholarship begins) a degree equivalent to a UK Upper Second-Class Honours degree
* Have completed NYSC by September 2026
* Be a Nigerian resident in Nigeria
* Not have previously studied in the UK or other developed countries
* Be aged 21–28 years by 30 September 2026
* Have a Nigerian international passport valid until December 2026





**Scholarship Benefits**

The scholarship covers:

* Full tuition fees
* One economy return flight ticket between Nigeria and the UK
* Contribution towards living expenses



**Deadline:** June 8, 2026
