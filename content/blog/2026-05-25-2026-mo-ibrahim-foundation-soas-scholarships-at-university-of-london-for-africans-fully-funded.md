Mo Ibrahim Foundation is offering a fully funded scholarship opportunity for Africans to study at the SOAS University of London in the United Kingdom.





The Mo Ibrahim Foundation SOAS Scholarship includes a £45,479 grant awarded to help selected candidates cover the costs of tuition and living stipends for the duration of study.



The Mo Ibrahim Foundation, an African organisation founded and funded by Dr Mo Ibrahim and dedicated to strengthening leadership and governance across the continent, has generously established a new scholarship at SOAS. 

This award aims to support and nurture future leaders committed to Africa’s development.





**Mo Ibrahim Foundation Scholarship Requirements:**

To be considered for the Mo Ibrahim Foundation Scholarship, you must:



* Be a newly admitted student, starting in September 2026.
* Be an Overseas fee status applicant.
* Have a minimum 2:1 bachelor’s degree from a UK university or international equivalent.
* Hold an unconditional offer for their programme by the time the scholarship panel meet.
* Mo Ibrahim Foundation SOAS Scholarship Duration and Reward
* There are two awards available for 2026/27, each with a total value of £45,479.
* Tuition fees will be deducted from this amount with the remainder being paid directly to the student as a maintenance stipend.



**Required Documents for Mo Ibrahim Foundation Scholarship 2026**

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;



* A copy of the unconditional offer received from SOAS admissions office.
* CV (please include your email address).
* Two pages max statement outlining knowledge and interest in governance-related issues.
* Your research proposal (as sent to the SOAS Admissions Office).
* One academic reference (to be sent directly by referee ab17@soas.ac.uk).





**Deadline**: June 17, 2026
