 Applications are now open for the **Global Youth Empowerment Fund (YEF) 2026,** a funding opportunity designed to support young changemakers and youth-led initiatives creating positive social impact in communities around the world. 

The initiative is part of the Global Youth Mobilization, a global movement led by major youth organisations in partnership with international institutions to empower young people to drive meaningful change.

**About the Fund**

The Youth Empowerment Fund (YEF) provides micro-grants to youth-led projects addressing urgent local and global challenges.

The programme supports initiatives focused on:

• Community development

• Climate action

• Education and skills development

• Health and wellbeing

• Social inclusion and equality

The goal is to help young people design and implement practical solutions that create measurable impact within their communities.

**Why This Fund Matters**

Many young people have innovative ideas but lack access to funding and support to bring them to life. The Youth Empowerment Fund helps bridge that gap by giving youth-led initiatives the resources, visibility, and global network needed to scale their impact.

**Eligibility Criteria**

The fund is open to:

• Young people aged 14–30 years

• Youth-led organisations, groups, or individuals

• Applicants with innovative ideas addressing community challenges

• Young changemakers committed to social impact

Projects must be youth-led and community-focused.

**Program Benefits**

Successful applicants will receive:

• Micro-grant funding to implement their projects

• Opportunity to join a global youth network

• Increased visibility for their initiatives

• Access to capacity-building and support resources

**Grant Value**

Selected applicants can receive:

• Up to €5,000 in non-repayable funding for their projects

**How To Apply**

Interested applicants should:

• Visit the official application portal

• Submit details of their project idea and impact plan

• Provide information about their team or organisation
