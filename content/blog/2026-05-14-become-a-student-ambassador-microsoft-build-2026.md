## Take your next big step now

Prepare for the future by building practical, in-demand skills that make you competitive and help you succeed in an AI-fluent workforce. Open to all students - no matter your background or field of study.

Student Ambassadors are curious and motivated—growing their skills with Microsoft technology. No matter where they’re starting from, they’re exploring new topics, learning out loud, and bringing others along with them.

Student Ambassadors bring people together—online and in their local communities. From hosting events to sharing what they’re learning, they create space for other students to connect and grow around Microsoft technology.

Student Ambassadors lead with purpose—they think ahead and take action. Whether building a career path or exploring a startup idea, they’re using what they’re learning today to create impact for tomorrow, knowing technology shapes what’s next.
