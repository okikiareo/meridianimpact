There is a significant possibility in Germany for international students who want to pursue a career in artificial intelligence. This scholarship may turn out to be one of the most alluring funding opportunity available in Germany for students looking for sponsored AI master’s programs in Europe without cumbersome sponsorship procedures. **SECAI** offers more than mere assistance with tuition. The award is intended to assist upcoming AI researchers by paying for living expenses in Germany, allowing students to concentrate on their studies, professional networking, and research growth. 



SECAI is a part of the esteemed national Zuse Schools program, which is supported by Germany’s Federal Ministry of Research, Technology, and Space and aims to develop the country’s future generation of AI researchers and innovators. SECAI promotes a broader approach to AI and actively welcomes applications from interdisciplinary backgrounds, such as engineering, medicine, psychology, law, computational sciences, and human-centered AI studies, in contrast to many scholarship programs that primarily target computer science students.



The program’s specific goal is to increase globalization and diversity in AI-related education. Special personal circumstances are taken into account during evaluation, and women in STEM disciplines are encouraged to apply. This covers candidates who have experienced political persecution, systemic disadvantages, displacement due to conflict, or refugee situations. Students interested in Germany’s research-based AI education system may find it advantageous to apply far in advance of the final deadline, since AI scholarships around Europe continue to become more competitive each year. **Financial benefits:**

Selected candidates by SECAI AI Masters Scholarship will be given:

* EUR 934 monthly stipend
* Potential extra family allowance for qualified overseas students
* Possibility of concentrating entirely on academic study and research in Germany
* Access to the expert AI research network at SECAI
* Exposure to multidisciplinary AI initiatives and partnerships
* Integration of research and academia in Germany’s AI environment

According to the scholarship regulations, students who earn more than EUR 603 gross per month may have their grant amount reduced following declaration.

> **Programs Offered:**

**At TU Dresden**:

* M.Sc. Computer Science
* M.Sc. Computational Modeling and Simulation (Applied Artificial Intelligence track)
* M.Sc. Computational Modeling and Simulation (Computational Life Science track)
* M.Sc. Nanoelectronic Systems

**At Leipzig University:**

* M.Sc. Data Science
* M.Sc. Bioinformatics
* M.Sc. Medical Informatics



**Documents Required:**

* An online application form
* Academic Transcript
* Degree Certificate
* A valid passport
* Updated CV or resume.
* Other Supporting documents



**Eligibility Criteria:**

* Applicants must be a master’s student at Leipzig University or TU Dresden, either now or in the future.
* Study in a field linked to AI that is related to SECAI research areas
* Exhibit outstanding academic achievement
* Typically have a converted German GPA of at least 1.7.
* If you haven’t enrolled yet, submit separate applications for scholarships and university entrance.
* Crucially, candidates can apply for the scholarship and their preferred master’s program at the same time. Both procedures continue to function independently.
* After completing the Grundstudium phase, students enrolled in German Diploma programs may also be eligible.

One of the SECAI scholarship’s main features is its emphasis on interdisciplinary AI studies. Students with the following backgrounds may also be eligible if their electives, thesis work, or research concentration is AI-related:

* Computer Science Master’s or Diploma Programs
* LL.M. programs focused on AI and data law.
* Social sciences and humanities programs that investigate the societal elements of AI
* Psychology programs that specialize in socio-technical systems and human-machine interaction.
* Engineering and computational sciences are linked to AI applications.
* Medical and healthcare informatics programs

This expanded definition of AI eligibility makes the award especially appealing to applicants whose research combines AI with ethics, healthcare, politics, human behavior, or advanced simulations.
