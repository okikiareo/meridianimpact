**The Etica ESG Fellowship** is a capacity-building program focused on people, profit, and planet. It trains changemakers to lead in Environmental, Social, and Governance solutions through research, sustainable finance, energy transition, and impact projects. Fellows work on real-world ESG challenges while connecting to global networks.

ESG is becoming big in Nigeria, from green energy startups to banks and NGOs reporting sustainability. This fellowship directly builds skills that Nigerian institutions and global markets are hiring for right now. Plus, the focus on Africa’s development goals and energy transition means your work can actually influence policy and projects back home. No prior ESG experience required to start, just commitment to impact.



**Who Can Apply**

Professionals, entrepreneurs, innovators, students, and youth in Nigeria working in or interested in ESG, climate action, renewable energy, sustainable finance, project management, health, water, and consulting. Both early-career and mid-level applicants are welcome.





***Eligible Countries***

***Open to Nigerians and other applicants globally.*** 





**Benefits**

* World-class curriculum on ESG strategy, sustainable finance, impact measurement, and systems thinking
* Mentorship from global ESG leaders and industry programs  
* Hands-on project to design and implement real ESG solutions
* Research publication, certificate, and recommendation letters
* Career navigation in climate, renewable energy, consulting, and project management
* Access to networks, collaborations, and visibility for your work
* Positioning for scholarships/awards and mentorship for travel experiences
* Training in Net Zero data analysis and research writing





**Deadline: August 31st, 2026**
