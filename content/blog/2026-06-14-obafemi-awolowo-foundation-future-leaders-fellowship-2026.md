**The Obafemi Awolowo Foundation is pleased to welcome applications from interested and qualified candidates for its 2026 Future Leaders Fellowship.**



The Obafemi Awolowo Foundation Future Leaders Fellowship offers an 8-week leadership journey for emerging leaders across Africa, the Caribbean, and the global diaspora.

The Ọbafẹmi Awolọwọ Future Leaders Fellowship Programme is a highly selective, merit-based initiative inspired by the enduring legacy of Chief **Ọbafẹmi Awolọwọ, SAN, GCFR (1909-1987).**

The programme is designed to nurture ethical and visionary young leaders aged 20–35 from Africa, the Caribbean, and the global diaspora, equipping them with the values, discipline, and perspective required for impactful leadership.





**Eligibility Requirements for Obafemi Awolowo Future Leaders Fellowship 2026**

To be considered for the Obafemi Awolowo Future Leaders Fellowship, you must:

* Be between age 25-30
* Be a citizen of Africa, the Caribbean, and the global diaspora.
* Have strong interest in leadership
* Be visionary
* Show strong leadership potential, integrity, and service orientation
* Be available for the full programme duration



**Benefits of Obafemi Awolowo Foundation Future Leaders Fellowship 2026**

The Obafemi Awolowo Future Leaders Fellowship offers;

* Leadership development
* Personal effectiveness training
* Mentorship
* Collaborative learning.



**Required Documents for Obafemi Awolowo Future Leaders Fellowship 2026**

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;

* Application form
* A personal statement
* Two Referees
