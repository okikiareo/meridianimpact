The programme provides talented young musicians with the opportunity to receive professional music education at the prestigious **MUSON** School of Music in Lagos, Nigeria.

Since inception in 2006 and in partnership with the Musical Society of Nigeria (**MUSON**), the premium music school in Nigeria, MTN Foundation has been providing fully funded scholarships to talented young Nigerians annually to acquire an internationally recognized Diploma in Music.



**Requirements**

* The MTN MUSON Music Scholars Programme is open to eligible talented young Nigerians who wish to improve their skills in Music.
* Interested candidates should have the following eligibility criteria:
* Minimum academic qualification: General Certificate of Education (GCE) ordinary level or Senior Secondary School Certificate (SSCE) with credits in five (5) subjects including English Language in not more than 2 sittings.
* Music Practical and Theory: Grade five (5) or higher pass certificate of the MUSON School of Music or any other music examining body recognized by the MUSON School such as MUSON, ABRSM or Trinity (London).
* Eligible Age criteria is 18–35 years.
