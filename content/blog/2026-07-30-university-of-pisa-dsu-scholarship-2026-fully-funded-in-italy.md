The DSU Toscana Scholarship is offered by the Tuscany Regional Government for students at University of Pisa. It’s a need-based, fully funded scholarship. 

Goal: Make sure money doesn’t stop you from studying at one of Europe’s oldest universities.





**Who Can Apply**

* Who: EU + Non-EU international students including Nigeria
* Level: Bachelor’s, Master’s, Specialization, PhD 
* Academic: Must be admitted to or already enrolled at University of Pisa
* Financial: Must prove family income is below threshold. For non-EU, you need embassy-certified income docs
* Other: Cannot already hold a degree at the same level you’re applying for





**Eligible Countries**

**All nationalities - EU and non-EU. Nigeria qualifies.**



**Benefits**

* Full tuition fee waiver
* Free accommodation in university residences OR rent subsidy
* Free meals in DSU canteens - 1 or 2 per day
* Annual stipend: €2,400 - €7,200 depending on student status
* Extra allowance for books, sports, activities
* Basic health insurance for non-EU students



**Documents needed:**

*  Passport + CV
*  Academic transcripts + degree certificate
*  Proof of family income - must be legalized/translated by Italian Embassy
*  Family composition certificate
*  Bank statement
*  Admission letter from University of Pisa





> *Italy is cheap to study in and DSU makes it FREE.* 
>

>
> *You get accommodation + food + cash. That’s rare in Europe.*
>
> *Many courses taught in English. No IELTS required for some programs.*



**Deadline: September 5, 2026**
