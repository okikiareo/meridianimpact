**The Kansas State University Scholarship 2026 will offer full tuition coverage, health insurance, and a competitive living stipend per year in forms of graduate teaching and research assistantships.** 


**2026 Kansas State University Scholarship Details:**


A fully funded scholarship is available for a highly motivated M.S. or Ph.D. student to begin Fall 2026 Spring 2027. The position will be housed within the Department of Agronomy under the direction of Dr. Colin Tobin.  Research in the program focuses on applied rangeland ecology and livestock grazing systems across the Great Plains. Current and developing projects include: 

1. Livestock foraging behavior and grazing distribution
2. Targeted grazing for invasive species management
3. Precision livestock technologies (GPS collars, virtual fencing, remote sensing)
4. Fire–grazing interactions in grassland ecosystems
5. Integrated livestock and wildlife interactions on working landscapes

The successful candidate will be expected to develop an independent research project while contributing to collaborative field research. Opportunities exist for interdisciplinary collaboration in range science, animal science, soil science, ecology, precision agriculture, and data analytics. 



**Eligibility Requirements for Kansas State University Scholarship 2026**
To be considered for the 2026 Kansas State University Scholarship, you must possess the following:


1. B.S. (for M.S. applicants) or M.S. (for Ph.D. applicants) in range science, animal science, natural resources, ecology, agronomy, wildlife biology, or a related discipline,
2. written and oral communication skills,
3. interest in field-based research and quantitative data analysis, experience with livestock systems, GIS, drone technology, or statistical software is beneficial but not required,
4. ability to work independently and as part of a research team, and 
5. work in adverse field conditions and traverse rugged terrain.



**Benefits of 2026 Kansas State University Scholarship**
The Kansas State University Scholarship includes graduate teaching and research assistantships that covers;
**tuition, living stipends per year, health insurance**
