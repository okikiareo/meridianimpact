A fully funded Masters degree opportunity is available for home and international students at the Minnesota State University (MSU) in the United States.

**The Minnesota State University Scholarship** offers a graduate opportunity that covers tuition and provides a living stipend of $20,000 per year for selected candidates.

A MS position is available (pending grant funding) in the Department of Biological Sciences to assess the response of curlyleaf pondweed and native plant communities to late season herbicide applications in southern Minnesota lakes.

The project is supervised by Dr Ryan Wersal’s research of the same department.

The project will be conducted in six lakes and will require extensive sampling from a boat and through the ice during winter.





**Minnesota State University Masters Scholarship Requirements:**

To be considered for the University of Minnesota Undergraduate Scholarship, you must:

* Have a Bachelors degree in a relevant field
* Experience with point intercept surveys, water sampling, and ArcGIS PRO would be greatly helpful.
* Meet the entrance requirements of the Minnesota State University
* A valid drivers license is required and experience trailering a boat is preferred.



**Minnesota State University Scholarship Benefits 2026**

The Minnesota State University Scholarship is a graduate research position that covers;

1. tuition fee coverage
2. a living stipend of $20,000 per year

 

**How To Apply for Minnesota State University Scholarship 2026**

To apply for the Minnesota State University Scholarship, interested and qualified candidates should;



* Submit the required documents (cover letter, CV, unofficial transcripts, and the names and contact information of 3 references) to Dr. Ryan M. Wersal at ryan.wersal@mnsu.edu.
* A formal application will be needed to Minnesota State University, Mankato after the initial interview process.
* Position start date: August 2026. The results of the grant submission will be known by July 2026 and the position is contingent upon this funding.





**Deadline: June 30, 2026**
