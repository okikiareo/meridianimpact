**The UNESCO Fellowships provides financial aid for students, PhD candidates, early-career professionals, and project initiators to foster growth and innovation across UNESCO’s areas of focus.**


Supported by donors and extrabudgetary funds, UNESCO provides more than 450 annual fellowships for research and practical training in UNESCO’s areas of focus. 

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (**UNESCO**), is a specialized agency dedicated to strengthening international cooperation in the fields of education, science, culture, and information.

Fellowships are available across PhD programs, innovative project ideas, and postdoctoral opportunities hosted by several institutions across several countries of the world.



#### Eligibility Requirements for UNESCO 2026 Fellowships

To be considered for the UNESCO 2026 Fellowships, you must:

* be students, professionals, early-career researchers, young professionals, or postdoctoral fellows.
* apply for an available program on the UNESCO Fellowship page.
* satisfy the criteria of the program you are applying to.
* be in good health, both mentally and physically.
* meet the admission requirements of the applied universities in terms of academic ability, language proficiency and other relevant criteria.

Benefits of UNESCO Fellowships 2026

* UNESCO Fellowships are usually fully the organization or in partnership with other organizations.
* Grants and awards are usually provided for fellows to support their studies, research, projects, or ideas.
* For the full period of study, training, or research, fellows are sponsored with financial aid that covers travel, research, housing, living, and other related costs.

Other benefits include;

* International training opportunities
* Global networking opportunities
* Access to globally ranked institutions
* Leadership development
* Technical training and professional mentorship
