The WomenPower2030 project has the overall objective to advance the rights and leadership of women, girls, and other marginalized persons in the framework of sustainable development.

The project’s expected outcome is increased engagement and influence of women´s and feminist Civil Society Organizations in the design and implementation of gender-just climate action and sustainable development policies, strategies, and programs at the national, regional, and global levels.



**WomenPower2030:Feminist Accelerate Action for Sustainable Development is a Financial Framework Partnership Agreement (FFPA) between European Union and a consortium of 5 feminist organizations led by Women Engage for a Common Future (WECF). Other consortium members are: Women Environmental Programme; African Women’s Development and Communication Network -FEMNET; Asia Pacific Forum for Women, Law and Development (APWLD); and Fundacion Para Estudio E Investigacion de la Mujer (FEIM).**



The women-led and women’s rights organizations are to use this seed-grants to design and implement initiatives that will contribute to the realization of the goal of the WomenPower2030 project. These initiatives should be designed, touching on one or a combination of the following areas:

* Policy monitoring and dialogue with policy makers
* Educational and awareness raising activities on gender and/or sustainable development
* Movement building
* Demonstrating equitable and sustainable solutions and services
* Gender specific trainings and monitoring/women leadership trainings
* Tracking SDG/climate policies and budgets
* Social media campaign and mainstream media activities

Note: Initiatives whose primary focus is only awareness raising and workshops will not be accepted. Priority will be given to initiatives that aim to influence policies in favour of women, demonstrate sustainable solution and services and ensure gender responsive budgets where women’s needs are integrated.



**Eligibility**

* You are a women-led and women’s rights organization
* Women make up at least 70% of your management team
* Your organization is affiliated with the Gender Advocacy Movement for the Environment (GAME) or any national, regional or global women’s movement or network.
* Your organization has not been convicted of fraud or any criminal activity
* Your organization has demonstrable capacity to effectively deliver on projects
* Your organization is based in Sierra Leone or Nigeria

**Duration of work**

* The duration of projects should be between 3 to 6 months

**Budget**

* The maximum amount for each initiative is 5,000 EUR

**Language of application**

* English language



**How to Apply**

* Applications should be submitted in Word (doc, docx) format with the following attachments:
* Detailed budget (Excel Sheet)
* Certificate of registration
* Letter of reference
* Track record of activities (e.g reports, media links, or testimonials from previous projects) compiled as a single document
* We may request more information from you should there be a need.
* Application should not be more than 10 pages (excluding attachments).

> Applications that do not address the specified areas of the assignment will not be considered.

Completed applications should be sent to **grants@wepnigeria.net** with the email subject line – **WomenPower2030 seed-grant application.**

*Note: Due to high volume of applications expected, only successful applicants may be contacted.*



 **Deadline: 31st July 2026, 23:59 WAT**
