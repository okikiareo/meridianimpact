Candidates for this paid internship with the FAO may be recent graduates or students enrolled in undergraduate or graduate degree programmes. The internship is offered by the United Nations Organization. It is an internship program, where you do not have to move to another country. Ideally, interns are placed at FAO offices or project locations inside their nation or residency. Students from all over the world can apply for this internship at the organization. The FAO paid internship programme 2026 period ranges from 3 to 11 months, depending on your assignment. It is offered by the UN government. 

The FAO program is for those candidates who want to bring a change in the world. It is an intergovernmental organization. If you are under financial or living constraints you can apply for this internship. It encourages students who are determined and hardworking. The internship will provide a wide chance for young and talented men and women to work from all over the world. It benefits the selected internship candidates to practice their fieldwork. The FAO job opportunity seeks to help you turn your ambitions into reality by exposing you to issues related to food.



The Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the United Nations is universally accepted. It was founded in 1945. There are 194 Member Nations in the FAO. It’s important motto is to end hunger in the world. Its headquarters are in Rome, Italy. The organization helps the government at the international level to develop forestry, agriculture and food resources. 



**Financial Benefits:**

FAO Internship Program 2026 is a Fully Funded Program for International Students. The details of coverage is given below:

1. This Internship Programme is offering a stipend of around $700 per month to the interns.
2. The internship will provide medical protection to the applicants.
3. The candidate will be provided with the opportunity to meet people with diverse thoughts and skills. They will come to know about the culture of the candidates worldwide.
4. The internship will provide you with an opportunity to exhibit your skills in your field of interest because you will be part of many projects in your field.

**Documents Required:**

1. You must submit your letter of motivation. This shows why you are participating in the FAO internship programme, as well as if you already have done some work with them you need to mention that too.
2. Make sure to include the additional supporting papers in the application form if necessary.
3. You have to submit evidence of enrollment in a recent degree in an undergraduate or graduate programme.
4. You must have to submit your language proficiency certificate.
5. Candidates must be in the country legally, either as residents or as immigrants.



**Eligibility Criteria** 

The candidates who are eligible for this opportunity should have to fulfill the following needs:

1. You must be a degree holder in the relevant field. You must be a recently graduated or undergraduate program. You must be a student of a well-known institute.
2. You must have the nationality of FAO countries.
3. You must have a good command of FAO official languages. These official languages include Arabic, English, Chinese, Spanish, French, and Russian.
4. FAO internship programme is age limited. Your age may start from 21 to 30 years old.
5. You must have a flexible attitude to adjust to a diverse cultural environment.
6. You must be good at communication skills.
7. You must be efficient enough in computer skills.





**Application deadline:**

The deadline for the FAO Internship Program 2026 opportunity has not been mentioned yet. You must apply now if you are interested in this opportunity.
