Are you an African entrepreneur ready to scale your business across borders, master global compliance, and secure institutional backing? This is your gateway to growth. Business Finance

234Finance, a leading platform promoting African entrepreneurship, has officially opened applications for the SME Bootcamp 2026. Designed to bridge the funding and knowledge gap for growing enterprises, this year’s bootcamp offers participants access to a $2 Million funding pool alongside world-class training and corporate resources.



**Why This Matters**

Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) are the primary engine of economic development across the African continent. However, sub-Saharan businesses frequently face steep hurdles when attempting to scale—ranging from complex cross-border trade regulations to a lack of investment readiness.

The SME Bootcamp 2026 addresses these critical pain points directly. By combining structured capacity building with substantial equity, debt, and grant opportunities, this initiative equips homegrown founders with the precise tools required to build resilient, investor-ready enterprises capable of competing on a global stage.



**Who Should Apply? (Eligibility Criteria)**

The bootcamp is specifically looking for structured African SMEs that meet the following benchmarks:

Business Longevity: 3+ years of active operations.

Financial Capacity: Up to $100,000 in annual turnover.

Team Size: A dedicated team of 2 to 10 people.



 **Program Benefits:** 

Selected businesses will undergo an intensive transformation, gaining direct access to tools, capital, and networks:

8 Weeks of Expert-Led Training: Master cross-border trade, export compliance, logistics, investor readiness, and operational scaling.

Mentorship & Business Evaluation: Engage directly with seasoned corporate leaders and industry experts for strategic guidance throughout the programme.

Access to $2M Funding Pool: Top-performing businesses will compete for grants, debt, and equity financing opportunities.

Free Perks & Infrastructure: Eligible businesses receive free trademark registration and AWS cloud credits to digitize operations.

Investment Readiness Tools: Free, unrestricted access to foundational investment assessments and readiness toolkits.

MMC 8.0 Exhibition Spot: Exceptional performers will secure a free exhibition space to showcase their businesses at 234Finance’s premier continental showcase. Business Finance
