The **Boston College Nursing Scholarships** are offered in the forms of fellowships, grants, teaching and research assistantships, and tuition remission.

The Boston College Connell School of Nursing offers programs prepare nursing professionals for fulfilling careers in which they work to transform health care and advance nursing science.

With a comprehensive curriculum, the school educates nurses who translate knowledge and research into practice, and our clinical placements throughout the New England region embed students in the heart of one of the nation’s best health care communities.

***From Bachelor’s, Master’s, Mid-wifery, Doctor of Nursing, and PhD programs, the Boston College School of Nursing Scholarships are open to candidates seeking undergraduate and graduate programs.***





**Eligibility Requirements for Boston College School of Nursing Scholarship**

To be considered for the Boston College School of Nursing Scholarship, you must:

1. be a home or an international student
2. apply for the BSc, MSc, DNP, or PhD programs available at the CSON.
3. meet the entry requirements of Boston College.
4. satisfy the English Language requirements of the institution.



**Benefits of Boston College Nursing Scholarship**

The Connell School of Nursing offers several funding opportunities to selected or admitted candidates. These include;

1. teaching and research assistantships
2. fellowships
3. scholarships
4. awards
5. grants
6. tuition remission

**Available Programs at Boston School of Nursing**

The available programs at the Boston School of Nursing includes;

1. Bachelor’s Programs
2. Master’s Programs
3. Master of Science, Nurse-Midwifery
4. Doctor of Nursing Practice Program
5. D.N.P. in Nurse Anesthesia Program
6. Ph.D. Program
7. Professional and Continuing Education



 

**How to Apply for Boston College Nursing Scholarships 2026 (Step by Step)**

To apply for the Boston College Nursing Scholarship, interested and qualified candidates should;

1. Apply for one of the available programs at the Connell School of Nursing.
2. Upload the required documents and submit your application.
3. Successful candidates will be offered scholarships, assistantships, and other study opportunities.
