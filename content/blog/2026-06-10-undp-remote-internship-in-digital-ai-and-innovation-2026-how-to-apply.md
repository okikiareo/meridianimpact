The UNDP Digital Transformation Internship 2026 is open to Bachelor’s, Master’s Degree, and graduate students, and allows interns to use digital technology in UNDP’s development projects.

Selected applicants get a monthly stipend between $200 and $500 and the opportunity to mission to end poverty, build democratic governance, the rule of law, and inclusive institutions around the world.





The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) is looking for motivated interns who can support our work in any of the following areas listed below.

UNDP is the United Nations’ global sustainable development organization, working across 170 countries with 17,000 staff.   

Since the launch of its first Digital Strategy in 2019, UNDP has been on an ambitious transformation journey to harness the potential of digital technology for development.







**Eligibility Requirements for UNDP Digital Transformation Internship 2026**

To be considered for the UNDP Digital, AI and Innovation Internship 2026, you must:



1. Be enrolled in a graduate school programme (second university degree or equivalent, or higher), or. 
2. Be enrolled in the final academic year of a first university degree programme (undergraduate; minimum bachelor’s level or equivalent); or 
3. Have graduated with a university degree (first university degree, or second university degree or equivalent, or higher) and, if selected, must commence the internship within a one-year period of graduation. 
4. Interest/Experience in one of the workstream areas is desirable.



**Benefits of UNDP Digital, AI and Innovation Internship 2026**

The UNDP Digital, AI and Innovation Internship 2026 offers the following benefits;

In accordance with the UNDP Internship policy, UNDP interns are eligible to receive a monthly stipend, with the rate that varies depending on the duty location. The stipend will be paid monthly, and part-time.

Remote internship arrangements are prorated accordingly. 



**Required Documents for UNDP Remote Internship 2026**

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;

1. Letter from their university confirming current enrollment and graduation date.  
2. Copy of their most recent school transcript. 
3. Copy of passport. 
4. Visa copy- If the candidate is located in the USA (United States of America) 
5. Signed UNDP Internship Agreement. 
6. Medical Certificate of Good Health. 
7. Proof of Medical Insurance.



**Deadline: September 30, 2026**
