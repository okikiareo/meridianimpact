All International Students are eligible for Endeavour II Scholarship Scheme. Students can undertake their Masters Degree and PhD Degree programs under Malta Government Scholarship. The Endeavour scholarship aims to enhance the capabilities of individuals which will help towards Malta’s economic development. 



Students those who are selected for this scholarship will be exempted from paying tuition fees and will get a chance to study in leading higher education institutes in Malta. European Union is going o fund this scholarship awards for students from around the world. Malta Government is sure that the Postgraduate education plays an important role in any country’s economic and social agenda.



**Financial Benefits of Malta Government Scholarships:**

Malta Government Endeavour Scholarship 2026-27 in Malta will cover most of the expenses to Free study for postgraduate Degree programs. Details of the award coverage is given below:

* Scholarship will cover tuition fees, living expenses, and other essential costs.
* State-of-the-art facilities and innovative research opportunities.
* Admission to well known universities and renowned faculty members in Malta.
* Amazing opportunities for bachelors, postgraduate, and doctoral study students.
* A supportive learning community fostering academic excellence.
* Selected students will get membership in a prestigious alumni network for ongoing support and professional development.
* Cultural immersion in Malta’s rich heritage and vibrant society.
* International collaboration and networking opportunities.



**Eligibility Criteria:**

Students have to meet the given below criteria while apply for Malta Government Endeavour Scholarships Scheme 2026.

* The scholarship is targeted at Young students and adult learners to pursue studies at MQF Level 7 or MQF Level 8.
* MFQ Stands for the Malta Qualifications Framework (MQF)
* MQF Level 7= Master Studies
* MQF Level 8= PhD Studies
* Do not hold the same level of qualification.
* Prepared to start studies between May 15, 2026, and April 30, 2027.

**Note: If you already hold a Master’s degree then you are eligible to apply for the PhD, but not for another Master’s or lower-level postgraduate certificate or diploma. Applicants who are already enrolled in a PhD degree program or the same level of PhD are not eligible to apply for this scholarship.**



Application Deadline:

The last date to apply for Malta Government Endeavour Scholarships Scheme 2026-27 for International Applicants is 5th June 2026.

How You Can Apply for the Malta Endeavour Scholarship?

There is an Online Application Portal to apply for Malta Government Scholarship 2026 in Malta. Follow the given below steps to apply successfully. 

**Review Eligibility Criteria: First of all check that whether you meet the eligibility criteria.**

Prepare Application Materials: According to application guidelines prepare all the documents that required to apply for this scholarship.

Complete Application Form: Open “Apply Link” button given below. Fill out the official application form provided by the Malta Government Endeavour Scholarships Scheme. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about yourself and your academic background.

Submit Application: Submit your application along with all required documents before the specified deadline..

Await Review and Notification: After submitting your application, it will be reviewed by the scholarship selection committee. Wait for notification about the status of your application.
