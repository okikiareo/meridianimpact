The Marshall University Scholarship is a fully funded graduate research assistantship that includes a full tuition remission and a $21,000 stipend/year, plus a tuition waiver.



The Palmquist Lab at *Marshall University* is recruiting for a funded MS position at Marshall University in climate change and fire effects on coastal vegetation.

The research focuses on quantifying vegetation dynamics over space and time and identifying how the relative importance of ecological processes structuring plant communities changes with spatial and temporal scale. In addition, a key goal of our research is to understand how pattern and process in plant communities will be altered in the face of global environmental change.

The successful candidate will explore the interacting effects of sea-level rise and increased storm surge on coastal vegetation and the effects of prescribed fire on pine-dominated vegetation on Marine Corps Recruit Depot Parris Island (**MCRDPI**), a sea-island in South Carolina.





**Eligibility Requirements for Marshall University Scholarship 2026**

To be considered for the Marshall University Scholarship, you must meet the following requirements:

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:** 

A background, including relevant course work and/or research experience in plant ecology and fire ecology, strong communication skills, and strong quantitative skills.

Experience with remote sensing and GIS, and knowledge of the flora of the southeastern US are highly desirable.

 

**EXPECTATIONS:** 

The successful candidate will be expected to conduct high-quality research, serve as a teaching assistant within the Department of Biological Sciences, present their research to the scientific community at regional and national meetings, and publish in peer-reviewed scientific journals.





**Benefits of Marshall University Masters Scholarship 2026**

The Marshall University Masters Scholarship includes;

* $21,000/year
* a tuition waiver (excluding fees)
* The position is available starting August 2026 or January 2027.
* Funding will consist of a combination of research and teaching assistantships for two years. 





**Required Documents for Marshall University Scholarships 2026**

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;

* short statement of research interests and career goals (no longer than 1 page),
* CV, 
* unofficial transcripts, and
* contact information for three professional references.
