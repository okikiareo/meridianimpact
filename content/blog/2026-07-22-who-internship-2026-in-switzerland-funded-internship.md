**The World Health Organization (WHO) is** the specialized agency of the **United Nations** responsible for promoting global health, coordinating international responses to health emergencies, supporting disease prevention, and improving healthcare systems worldwide. Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, WHO works with governments, researchers, and international organizations to improve health outcomes across the globe. 

The **World Health Organization (WHO)** is inviting applications for its Health Emergencies Internship Programme, offering students and recent graduates the opportunity to gain practical experience in one of the world's leading international public health organizations. 

The  internship will take place at the **WHO** Headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland, for 24 weeks, running from 5 October 2026 to 19 March 2027. Selected  interns will receive a monthly living allowance of CHF 1,680, along with medical insurance, accident insurance, and daily lunch vouchers.



If you are passionate about global health, public health policy, medical research, or emergency preparedness, this internship provides valuable international experience while working alongside experts tackling global health emergencies.



**Financial Benefits**

Selected interns will receive:

1. Monthly living allowance of CHF 1,680
2. Daily lunch vouchers
3. Medical insurance coverage
4. Accident insurance
5. Practical experience at WHO Headquarters
6. International networking opportunities
7. Professional development in global public health





**Internship Responsibilities**

Interns will support the WHO Medical Countermeasures Unit by working on activities such as: 

1. Conducting literature reviews on medical countermeasures
2. Researching vaccines, diagnostics, therapeutics, and pandemic preparedness
3. Collecting and analyzing scientific data
4. Assisting with technical reports and policy briefs
5. Supporting international workshops, meetings, and conferences
6. Preparing background documents for WHO programs
7. Contributing to epidemic and pandemic preparedness projects
8. Assisting with research and technical documentation



**Learning Outcomes**

During the internship, participants will gain practical knowledge and experience in:

1. Global health operations
2. Public health emergency preparedness
3. Medical countermeasure strategies
4. Scientific research methodologies
5. Data collection and analysis
6. International health policy development
7. Technical report writing
8. Collaboration with international health professionals



**Eligibility Criteria**

Applicants should meet the following requirements:

* Open to applicants of all nationalities
* Have completed at least three years of undergraduate studies before the internship begins
* Be currently enrolled in a university degree program or
* Have graduated within the last 18 months

***Be studying or have studied in one of the following or related fields:***

1. Medicine
2. Public Health
3. Biology
4. Biochemistry
5. Microbiology
6. Virology
7. Bioinformatics
8. Statistics
9. Information Sciences
10. Medical Sciences

* Demonstrate strong English language proficiency
* Knowledge of French or another UN language is an advantage
* Possess good analytical, research, and communication skills
* Be familiar with Microsoft Office applications
