Nestle Plc in JAYA, Malaysia has called for applications for its 2026 Graduate Management Trainee Program.

Nestlé Management Trainee Program is an 18 to 24-month accelerated career initiative designed to develop recent university graduates into future business leaders.

Trainees undergo cross-functional rotations, intensive mentorship, and hands-on projects across departments like technical/engineering, production, quality assurance and application group.



#### Eligibility Requirements for Nestle Graduate Trainee Program 2026

**You will be the perfect candidate for the Nestle Graduate Trainee Program 2026 if you meet the following attributes:**

* Degree holder with a minimum CGPA of 3.2 or its equivalent
* Less than 2 years of working experience
* Excellent verbal and written English communication skills
* Strong in critical thinking skills, resilience and agility.
* Ability to work well independently and in a team





#### Benefits of Nestle Management Trainee Program 2026

The 2026 Nestle Management Trainee Program offers;

* competitive monthly stipend
* hands-on practical training
* career development opportunities
* completion certificate that upgrades your CV
* networking and learning opportunities from top industry professionals

#### Available Nestle Graduate Management Trainee Roles

The Graduate Management Trainee Roles are available across the following departments;

* **Engineering:**

   Focuses on optimizing production processes, implementing new technologies, and ensuring that our facilities operate at peak performance.
* **Production:**

   Involves managing the day-to-day operations of our manufacturing lines, ensuring that we meet production targets while adhering to safety and quality standards.
* **Quality Assurance:**

   Ensures that all products meet Nestlé’s stringent quality standards through rigorous testing and compliance checks, safeguarding our commitment to excellence.
* **Application Group:**

   Works closely with product development and marketing teams to create innovative solutions that meet consumer needs and preferences.
* **Finance:**

   Experience the function that drives the Company’s long-term profitable growth through strategic decision making, work closely with the different businesses and support functions to achieve business goals.
* **Marketing:**

   

  Build your brand management fundamentals through effective consumer targeting, new product development, consumer insights understanding, impactful creative communication, and marketing strategies in developing a successful brand.
* **Supply Chain:**

   

  Be a part of the value chain that manages product delivery to retail shelves, ensuring they are planned, produced and delivered in full, on time, and in fresh condition through timely demand and supply planning, strategic procurement, warehousing and transportation.
