The **Australian Government annually funds the University of Queensland Earmark Scholarship** for interested and qualified international and domestic postgraduate students to study and research in Australia.



The University of Queensland Earmark Scholarship will cover the full cost of tuition fees and will also provide a stipend of $37,500 per annum for successful students.



The **Earmarked** scholarships are funded by the Australian Government to assist eligible PhD students with their living costs and tuition fees. The scholarships are available to both international and domestic students to undertake a PhD study at the University of Queensland



The Earmarked scholarships are offered for research projects aligned with recently awarded Category 1 Research Grants. They offer PhD students the opportunity to work with leading researchers and contribute to large projects of national significance.





**University of Queensland Earmark Scholarship Requirements**

To be considered for the University of Queensland Earmark Scholarship, you must:

* meet the University of Queensland’s minimum entry into entry requirements for a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD).
* Be a domestic (Australian) or an international student
* meet the specific requirements of the project you are applying for.



**Benefits of University of Queensland Earmark Scholarship**

* The award covers the full tuition fees of successful candidates and will provide a living stipend of  $37,500 per annum tax free (reviewed annually) for 3.5 years
* The scholarship also covers the health insurance of selected beneficiaries
