The University of Oxford Wolfson College Scholarships cover payment of course fees and a £20,780 living stipend for the recipients pursuing Doctoral degrees.



**Wolfson College** is pleased to advertise up to 20 fully funded scholarships for DPhil and Master’s programmes starting in the 2026-27 academic year.

Many of Wolfson’s scholarships are partnered with the Oxford University Clarendon Fund. Students applying for UKRI (UK Research and Innovation) graduate funding may also be considered for a Wolfson-partnered UKRI award. 

Preference will be given to applicants who have listed Wolfson as their first-choice college when two (or more) candidates are of equal academic merit.





**Requirements for 2027 University of Oxford Wolfson College Scholarships**

To be considered for the University of Oxford Wolfson College Scholarships, you must:

1. Have completed a Bachelor’s degree from a recognized university.
2. Apply for an eligible DPhil program.
3. Meet the English Language requirements of the University of Oxford.
4. Meet the entrance requirements of the program you will be applying for. 
5. Benefits of University of Oxford Wolfson Scholarship 2027
6. All the University of Oxford Wolfson Scholarships cover payment of course fees and a living stipend for the recipient. 
7. Stipends of £20,780 at the UKRI rate per year for selected candidates.
