The Chinese Scholarship Council is pleased to offer eligible international applicants the China Link Scholarship Program, offering a valuable opportunity for young talents around the world to pursue academic and research activities in China.

The China Link Scholarship 2026 is a fully funded award that includes a tuition waiver, free on-campus accommodation, comprehensive medical insurance, and a stipend of CNY42,000.l



In order to deepen the academic friendship and mutual understanding between Chinese and foreign young generation, and to build a platform for the international exchanges and cooperation of young talents.

**China Scholarship Council (CSC) has established the China Link Scholarship Program for international students and faculties hoping to engage in academic and research activities in China.**





**Eligibility Requirements for China Link Scholarship Program 2026**

To be considered for the China Link Scholarship Program 2026, you must:

* Be a citizen of a country other than the People’s Republic of China, good moral character and academic excellence, be in good health, both mentally and physically.
* Be under 45 years of age when applying for general scholars.
* Be under 50 years of age when applying for senior scholars.
* Be registered as a full-time student or employed as a full-time academic staff at CSC’s foreign partner universities at the time of application and throughout their proposed stay in China.
* Have received either the admission notice or the invitation letter issued by the Chinese host university. 





**Benefits of China Link Scholarship Program**

Successful applicants will receive a full scholarship from CSC during their stay in China, including;

* a tuition waiver,
* free on-campus accommodation,
* comprehensive medical insurance and
* monthly stipend (CNY 3,000 for general scholars; CNY 3,500 for senior scholars). 





 

**How to Apply for China Link Scholarship 2026 (Step by Step)**

To apply for the China Link Scholarship Program, interested and qualified candidates should follow the procedures below;

* Applicants should contact the Chinese host university to obtain admission documents or invitations.
* Applicants should log into the Chinese Government Scholarship Information System (https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn/#/login) to complete CSC online application and upload supporting documents.
* Application will be processed by Chinese host universities.
* Chinese universities should submit scholarship nomination list to CSC at least 2 months before the start of study. The submission time could be flexible according to each university’s enrollment and admission arrangements.
* CSC notifies scholarship admission result to Chinese host universities.
* Chinese host universities will notify the scholarship admission results to applicants. 





**Deadline: Open All Year**
