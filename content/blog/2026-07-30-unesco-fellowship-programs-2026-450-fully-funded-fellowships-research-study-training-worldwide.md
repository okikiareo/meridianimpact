UNESCO offers over 450 fellowships every year supported by donors and partner countries.

Goal: Support students, researchers, PhD candidates, early-career professionals and project leaders in Education, Science, Culture, Communication, and Social Sciences. These aren’t just for study. Some are for research, training, or launching innovative projects. Most are fully funded.

UNESCO fellowships are prestigious and fully funded. Great if you want to study/research abroad without the cost. China, Poland, Korea options mean you’re not stuck with just US/UK.

They prioritize Africans and women, and cover fields like education, tech, culture, and science that matter here at home. 







**Who Can Apply**

* Who: Students, PhD candidates, early-career professionals, researchers, project initiators
* Level: Undergraduate, Master’s, PhD, Postdoc, Visiting Scholar
* Fields: Education, Natural Sciences, Engineering, Culture & Heritage, Communication, Social & Human Sciences
* Countries: Open to developing countries. Nigeria is eligible for most programs
* Note: Women, Africans, and SIDS candidates strongly encouraged





***Eligible Countries***

***Global - 135+ developing UNESCO Member States. Nigeria qualifies.***





**Top UNESCO Fellowships for 2026**

* UNESCO/China Great Wall Fellowships - 75 fellowships

   Master’s, PhD, Visiting Scholar in China. Full funding. Deadline: Jan 23, 2026

* UNESCO/Japan Young Researchers Fellowships Keizo Obuchi - For young researchers from developing countries.   Deadline: Sept 30, 2026
* UNESCO/Poland Archaeology & Conservation Fellowships - 9-month internship in Poland. 7 slots only
* UNESCO/Korea Co-Sponsored Fellowships - 25 fellowships
* TWAS Fellowships - 160 PhD fellowships
* TWAS Postdoctoral Fellowships - 100 fellowships 





**Benefits**

Depends on program, but typically:

* Full tuition coverage
* Monthly stipend/living allowance
* Accommodation support
* Round-trip airfare
* Medical insurance
* Research grants + Conference funding
* Networking + Mentorship with global experts 





**Deadline:**

**Varies by program**

Key ones:

* Great Wall China: Jan 23, 2026
* Poland Archaeology: *May 15, 2026
* Japan Young Researchers: Sept 30, 2026 





**How to Apply**

* Check UNESCO Fellowships portal for open calls
* Important: For Great Wall China, you must apply through Nigerian National Commission for UNESCO. Individual applications not accepted
* Prepare: CV, transcripts, research proposal, recommendation letters d8d8
