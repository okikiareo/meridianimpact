The fellowship supports innovative education, training, and communication initiatives that promote the efficient use of plant nutrients and accelerate the adoption of sustainable soil fertility management practices in African agricultural systems.



**FELLOWSHIP FOCUS AREA**

Applicants are encouraged to propose projects aligned with one or more of the following priority areas:

* 4R Nutrient Stewardship
* Nutrient and Water Management for Climate-Resilient Agriculture
* Soil Health, Nutrient Management and Diagnostics
* Precision Farming
* Farmer-led On-farm Innovation
* Projects that demonstrate strong collaboration and partnerships will receive favourable consideration.



**Eligibility Criteria**

Applicants must:

* Be full-time scientists, extension specialists, or educators.
* Be employed by an African National Agricultural Research and Extension System (NARES), university, non-profit organisation, or private sector institution.
* Be working on initiatives that improve soil fertility management in Africa.
* Submit an innovative outreach or education proposal.
* Students are not eligible for this fellowship.
* Female applicants are especially encouraged to apply.



**FUNDING**

Successful applicants will receive an award of USD 5,000 to support activities such as:

* Training programmes and workshops
* Development of communication and educational materials
* Knowledge-sharing initiatives
* Data synthesis and dissemination
* Travel related to outreach activities
* Implementation of innovative soil fertility management practices

 



**Application Requirements**

Applicants will need to submit: African business platform

* A proposal describing how the fellowship will improve outreach or implementation of soil fertility management practices.
* Two signed letters of support, including one from their supervisor.
* A current résumé or CV highlighting academic qualifications and relevant publications or communication products.
* Supporting documents in English or French.
* Successful fellows will also be required to submit a short report at the end of the award period outlining how the funds were used and the outcomes achieved.



**How to Apply**

Interested and eligible applicants should submit their applications through the official African Plant Nutrition Institute (APNI) application portal.

**Apply here**: https://www.apni.net/outreach-fellowship-apply/
