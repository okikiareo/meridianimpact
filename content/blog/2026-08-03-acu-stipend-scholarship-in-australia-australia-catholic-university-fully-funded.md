The Australian Catholic University offers stipend scholarships to support domestic and international candidates undertaking a Research Doctorate or Research Masters degree.

Applying for a research degree at ACU is an exciting opportunity to deepen your expertise, pursue original research, and work alongside experienced researchers and supervisors.

These scholarships are funded by the Australian Catholic University and awarded and administered in accordance with the ACU Research Training Program Scholarship Policy.





#### Eligibility Requirements for Australia Catholic University Scholarship 2027

To be considered for the Australia Catholic University, you must:

* be a domestic student or an overseas student enrolled in an accredited research higher degree at ACU.
* have proposed research that aligns with any of ACU’s broad 

  [areas of expertise](https://www.acu.edu.au/research-and-enterprise/our-people)

  .
* have strong academic credentials and research potential.

#### Benefits of ACU Stipend Scholarship 2027

Successful applicants will be awarded a scholarship package consisting of:

* Stipend scholarship of 

  **AUD $34,315**

  * per annum (full-time)
* Full fee offset valued at approximately 

  **AUD $34,728**

   per annum
* A single 

  [Overseas Student Health Care Policy](https://www.acu.edu.au/research-and-enterprise/graduate-research-school/scholarships/rtp-allowance-oshcp)

   and 

  [relocation allowance](https://www.acu.edu.au/research-and-enterprise/graduate-research-school/scholarships/rtp-allowance-relocation)

   of up to 

  **AUD $2,000**

   for international applicants.



#### How to Apply for ACU Stipend Scholarship 2027 (Step by Step)

The ACU Stipend Scholarship application process includes six key stages:

1. **Prepare –**

    Develop your research idea, gather documents, and organise referees or supervisors.
2. **Apply –**

    Submit your application and supporting documents.
3. **Referee reports –**

    Your nominated referees complete and submit their reports to support your application.
4. **Assessment –**

    ACU reviews your application and supporting materials.
5. **Outcome –**

    You’ll receive an outcome.
6. **Enrolment –**

    Accept your offer, complete enrolment steps, and prepare to commence your research degree.



#### Deadline: October 1, 2026
