As a beneficiary of the Olubi and Sarah Johnson Foundation Scholarship, your tuition fees, monthly stipend, accommodation, books and other materials will be proudly sponsored.

The Olubi & Sarah Johnson Foundation is pleased to announce a full scholarship for deserving undergraduate students who have been offered admission to study a STEM course at the University of Ibadan, Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria.



The **Olubi and Sarah Johnson Foundation (OSJ)** was founded in 2018 by Olubi Johnson (PhD) and his wife, Pastor Sarah Olubi Johnson (PhD). The Foundation was founded as a vehicle for them to give back to society as well as contribute to the enhancement and advancement of public education especially in their immediate community and in Nigeria at large.

This scholarship is the couple’s commitment to ensuring that financial hardship does not stand between a brilliant young mind and their divine destiny.





**Eligibility Requirements for OSJ Foundation Scholarship 2026**

To be considered for the OSJ Foundation Scholarship, you must:

1. Have scored 300 and above in the JAMB Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME)
2. Have been offered admission to study an undergraduate STEM course at the University of Ibadan
3. Are unable to fund their education due to low income or financial hardship







**Benefits of Olubi and Sarah Johnson Foundation Scholarship**

The Olubi and Sarah Johnson Foundation Scholarship covers;

1. Assigned University Fees
2. Accommodation
3. Books Support
4. Monthly Sustenance Stipend





 

**How to Apply for Olubi and Sarah Johnson Foundation Scholarship (Step by Step)**

To apply for the Olubi and Sarah Johnson Foundation Scholarship, interested and qualified candidates should;

1. Complete the application form correctly and save your application.
2. As part of the application, you will be required to write a “Brief Statement of Financial Need”.
3. Submit your application and await the selection from the foundation.
