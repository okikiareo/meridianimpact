The offer is announced by the **Adidas** Major Headquarters. Adidas also offers a Summer Internship for MBA students. All international students worldwide are invited to benefit from this offer. The job will give a great chance to global candidates to get a worthy experience at Adidas. The duration of this is a 12 weeks long period for MBA students. 
Adidas offers internships in a variety of fields, including design, marketing, supply chain management, and finance. Interns may work at the company’s headquarters in Herzogenaurach, Germany, or one of its regional offices around the world. Candidates can submit their online applications for internships at Adidas through the company’s careers website at any time of the year.

Adidas was founded in 1949. It is one of the largest sportswear manufacturers in the world, with operations in over 160 countries and a workforce of more than 60,000 people. Adidas is a global sportswear and lifestyle brand that designs and produces athletic footwear, apparel, and accessories. 

**Financial Benefits:**
In the Adidas Internships 2026 the candidates will be profited with the following:

Selected candidates will get a Monthly Stipend.
The applicant must enjoy a complete one-year contract with Adidas if s/he is enrolled in the Summer program.
The candidates will benefit from the experience of getting knowledge about the biggest brand market in the world.
The candidates will do work under the supervision of mentors.


**Documents Required:**
Educational certificates.
Updated CV (Resume)



**Eligibility criteria:**
The candidate must be a graduate or a student who is eligible to work for Adidas.
Having enrolled in any university program.
You must be enrolled in an undergraduate, graduate, or MBA program.
You must have applied for the internship by fulfilling all the requirements.
You must have a study in a relevant field.




**Application deadline:**
If you are interested in the Adidas Internships 2026 program you must have to apply at any time. There is no deadline to apply for this.
