**The Young Professionals Program (YPP)** is a flagship talent acquisition, development and management initiative aimed at building a pipeline of future experts for the Bank. The program targets exceptional, high-potential young professionals who are passionate about Africa’s development and are ready to contribute meaningfully to the Bank’s mission.



Designed as a three-year leadership development opportunity, the YPP offers a unique platform for qualified individuals from AfDB member countries to gain hands-on experience, rotate across different Departments, and prepare for impactful careers in development. It combines rigorous professional development with exposure to the Bank’s strategic operations and policy advisory work.



The Bank is committed to diversity and inclusion, and encourages qualified citizens from the Bank Group’s member countries from all backgrounds, to apply. As an equal opportunity employer, the African Development Bank Group strives to maintain a workforce that reflects its diverse membership.





**Eligibility requirements**

To join us, you should:

1. Be a citizen of a member country of the Bank.

2. Be 32 years of age or younger by the date of application.

3. Possess a master's degree or equivalent in any discipline that is relevant to the business of the Bank, with outstanding academic credentials.

4. Have at least 3 years' work experience in areas related to the Bank's high priority areas (Hi-5s).

5. Demonstrable hands-on experience on the African continent and/or in other continent's developing countries is a necessary requirement.

6. Have a passion for Africa's development.

7. Able to leverage knowledge, share and coach others. Work effectively in teams with displayed high adaptability to a diverse environment.

8. Proven teamwork, negotiation, client orientation, communication, strategic perspective, leadership and project management skills.

9. Strong analytical skills, entrepreneurial drive, results-orientation and problem-solving capability.

10. High business acumen and innovative mindset.

11. Be proficient in the Bank's working languages, either English and/or French.

12. Have skills that enable you work in a digital environment and embrace technology as it evolves.

13. Be able to use the Bank's standard software packages (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Access); knowledge of SAP is desirable.

14. Be willing to live in Abidjan, Cote d'Ivoire the Bank's Headquarters, and any other country in Africa where the Bank may require your services.

15. Ability to travel continentally when required.



 The qualifications, knowledge, and skills which the Bank will seek will be based on its identified strategic objectives and skills requirements of the Bank in planning for the Recruitment and Selection of each Cohort.



Please note that application for admission to the AfDB YPP is an online process. The YPP is highly competitive program, and you are advised to provide as much relevant information as possible.
