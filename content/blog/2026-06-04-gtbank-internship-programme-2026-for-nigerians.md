**GTBank** Internship Programme offers a paid opportunity to **graduates** to gain on-the-job training in Banking operations by supporting the day-to-day activities of GTB’s Transaction Services team.

The GTBank OND Internship Programme offers a paid opportunity for young graduates to kickstart their careers and is a great way to obtain basic knowledge about Banking operations.





**GTBank OND Internship Programme Details:**

Applications are invited from interested and qualified candidates to apply for the GTBank OND Internship Programme 2026.

The GTBank Internship Programme is a great way to obtain basic knowledge about several banking operations across the continent.

Guaranty Trust Bank (now GTCO) was the most profitable banking group in Nigeria with best-in-class Return on Equity and other key financial metrics in 2020.





**Eligibility Requirements for GTBank Internship Programme 2026**

To be considered for the GTB Internship Programme, you must possess the following:

1. OND Certificate/Notification of Results.
2. One-year Industrial Training Letter from an Accredited Polytechnic.
3. Guarantor Forms, Copy of Guarantor Staff ID Card.
4. \-Only One Guarantor is required if the Guarantor is a GTBank staff on minimum of ABO (grade)
5. \-Two Guarantors are required if the Guarantor is a non GTBank staff. These Guarantors must work with reputable companies on a full-time basis.
6. Valid Student ID Card, Recent Passport Photograph (White background only).
7. Birth certificate issued by National Population Commission.
8. Senior School Certificate Examination (WAEC/NECO) with at least a credit pass in English Language, Mathematics and any other three relevant subjects.
9. Good communication skills, basic numerical skills and a willingness to learn.



**Benefits of GTB Internship Programme 2026**

1. Montly Stipends
2. Other attractive bonuses 
3. The duration of the GTBank OND Internship is 12 months.
