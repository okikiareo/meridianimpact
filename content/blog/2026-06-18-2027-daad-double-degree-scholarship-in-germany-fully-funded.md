The German Academic Exchange Service presents an incredible opportunity through its DAAD Double Degree Scholarship for international students.



The 2027 DAAD Double Degree Scholarship is designed for highly qualified students who wish to study at a German and a foreign university, ultimately earning degrees from both institutions.



The **2027 DAAD Double Degree Scholarship** aims to develop or implement international degree programs with joint curricula from the German and foreign universities for groups of highly qualified German and foreign students.

This program is for applicants who study alternately at the German and foreign partner universities and both obtain national degrees (joint degree or double degree).

The DAAD Integrated international study programs with double degrees are intended to strengthen the exchange between teachers and students and make a lasting contribution to the development, expansion and consolidation of international structures at German universities.





**Eligibility Requirements for 2027 DAAD Double Degree Scholarship**

The DAAD Double Degree Program is open to state and state-recognized German universities.

Bachelor’s and Master’s students, along with faculty members from participating institutions, are eligible to apply. However, double degree programs in France are excluded, as these are supported by the Franco-German University (DFH).



**Benefits of DAAD Double Degree Scholarship**

* Development of Curriculum: Crafting a joint curriculum that allows students to study alternately at the German and partner universities.
* Mobility Grants: Scholarships for students, covering living expenses and travel between the partner institutions.
* Support for Faculty: Mobility grants for professors and guest lecturers involved in the program.
* Public Relations: Promoting the program through various channels to attract top talent.



 

**How to Apply for DAAD Double Degree Scholarship 2027 (Step by Step)**

*To apply for the DAAD Double Degree Scholarship, interested and qualified applicants should;*

* Applications must be submitted via the DAAD online portal.
* Ensure all required documents are in place, including cooperation agreements and curricular concepts.
* The program supports a diverse and inclusive environment, so applications that align with these values are highly encouraged.



**Deadline: October 15, 2026**
