**The OIST Scholarship Program is a fully funded opportunity hosted by the Okinawa Institute of Science & Technology in Japan.**

The OIST PhD Program covers all costs of study, living, and research expenses for selected international candidates to study and conduct research with some of the world’s best facilities.



The **Okinawa Institute of Science & Technology Graduate School** accepts about 60 full-time graduate students per year to deliver the best-in-the-world teaching to our graduate students.

The OIST Scholarship Program is for students who are currently enrolled in their last two years of a bachelor’s degree, enrolled in any year of a master’s degree, or who have graduated with a bachelor’s or master’s degree.



The Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) is an interdisciplinary graduate school offering a 5-year PhD program in Science.







**Eligibility Requirements for OIST PhD Program**

To be considered for the OIST PhD Program, you must:

* be enrolled in their last two years of a bachelor’s degree, enrolled in any year of a master’s degree, or have graduated with a bachelor’s or master’s degree.
* Submit an application at any time before each application deadline.
* Meet the English Language proficiency requirements of OIST.





**Benefits of OIST Scholarship Program 2027**

OIST is a Special Private School Corporation, funded mostly by the Japanese government.

* The OIST Scholarship Program provides financial support that covers tuition and living expenses, allowing students to focus on completing their degrees and contributing to the research output of the university.



**Required Documents for OIST PhD Program**

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;



* Application Form
* Transcript and Diploma
* Statement of Purpose
* Recommendation Letter
* English Proficiency Score (Optional)
* Passport
* ID Photo

 

**Deadline: November 15, 2026**
