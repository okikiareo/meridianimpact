**The Beiersdorf NIVEA Global** Graduate Program offers international exposure, hands-on experience, leadership development, and cross-functional training for young graduates to kickstart their careers in Germany.


Applications are now open for the Beiersdorf Global Graduate Programme in Germany.

The program provides an international graduate opportunity for young professionals interested in supply chain management, logistics, operations, and global business systems.

Participants will gain international exposure, hands-on experience, leadership development, and cross-functional training within a global supply chain environment.

#### Eligibility Requirements for Beiersdorf NIVEA Global Graduate Program

To be considered for the Beiersdorf NIVEA Global Graduate Program, you must satisfy the following requirements:

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in relevant discipline
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
* Interest in supply chain and operations management
* Good communication and teamwork abilities
* Leadership potential and initiative mindset
* Structured and solution-oriented thinking
* Strong digital affinity and project management interest
* Passion for innovation and operational excellence





#### Benefits of Beiersdorf Global Graduate Programme 2026

Selected Beiersdorf (NIVEA) Global Graduate Programme will enjoy the following benefits;

* International working opportunities
* Competitive remuneration
* Leadership and mentorship programmes
* Professional development support
* Flexible and hybrid work arrangements
* Networking opportunities
* Transportation support
* Health and wellness programmes
* Sports and leisure activities
* Career growth within a global company
* Exposure to world-class supply chain operations

#### Roles and Responsibilities of Beiersdorf Global Graduate Program

Participants will engage in the following activities while undergoing the graduate program;

* Participate in operational and strategic supply chain functions
* Support process improvement initiatives
* Work on logistics and transportation operations
* Contribute to demand planning and procurement projects
* Collaborate with international and cross-functional teams
* Support innovation-driven supply chain solutions
* Participate in business rotations and project ownership
* Assist future-ready and sustainable supply chain initiatives
