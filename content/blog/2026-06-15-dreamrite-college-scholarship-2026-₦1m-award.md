The **Dreamrite** College Scholarship is an initiative that aims to provide educational opportunities to young people in Africa to secure their future. 

Each recipient of the Dreamers College Fund Scholarship will get a **₦1m** fund to support their tuition, accommodation, and living stipends.



The Dreamers College Fund Scholarship is aimed at equipping the leaders of tomorrow with the tools necessary to prepare them for greatness and opportunities that await them in the future. 

**DreamRite** is a charity organization that provides scholarship opportunities to young Africans to secure their future through education, the right knowledge, and exposure.

One of the Dream Rite Foundation is to provide academic support to students by providing funds that will support their academic endeavors.





**Eligibility Requirements for Dreamers College Fund Scholarship 2026**

To be considered for the **Dreamrite** College Scholarship, you must:

* Applicants may be high school graduates or first-year university students.
* Must be a citizen of the country where the scholarship program is offered.
* Applicants must be less than 25 years old at the time of the application.
* Applicants who are 25 years and older are not eligible.
* Applicants with university admission but yet to resume university education.
* Applicants who have met university admission requirements and have a high chance of getting university admission within 12 months of the scholarship award.
* The scholarship covers only degree or diploma programs (including OND, HND)



**Benefits of Dreamrite College Scholarship 2026**

If selected for the 2026 Dreamers College Fund  Scholarship, you will be awarded a ₦1m fund to help cover the cost of your study at any Nigerian accredited university.



**Required Documents for Dream College Fund Scholarship**

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;

* Complete Application Form
* A recent photograph. Upload must be in JPG or PNG formats.
* 3 essays with each consisting of a minimum of 500 words. All essay responses must
* be typed or pasted in the provided text field on the application.

If your application qualifies for Level II Review, you will be required to provide the following additional documents as part of your application;

* A copy of high school final year academic report or most recent academic report
* A letter of recommendation from your high school teacher or school representative
* JAMB, WAEC, or NECO results (if applicable, but not mandatory)



 

**How to Apply for Dreamrite College Scholarship 2026 (Step by Step)**

To apply for the Dreamrite College Scholarship, interested and qualified applicants should follow the steps below;

* Create and verify an account through your valid email address
* Input your personal details and upload required documents
