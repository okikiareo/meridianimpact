Applications are open for the Green Africa Associates SDGs Innovations Fellowship 2026, a fellowship that empowers young Africans to develop innovative solutions that contribute to achieving the 17 United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**. 

The fellowship seeks to identify and support young people with innovative ideas or solutions that can address social, economic, and environmental challenges across Africa, with a strong focus on innovation, green entrepreneurship, and sustainable development.





**Who Can Apply?**

The fellowship is open to young Africans who:

1. Are between 18 and 35 years old
2. Are citizens of an African country
3. Have an interest in innovation and problem-solving
4. Have a passion for green entrepreneurship and sustainability
5. Are committed to advancing the SDGs in their communities or sectors
6. Have an innovative idea, project or solution capable of creating meaningful impact
7. The programme welcomes applicants from diverse academic, professional and entrepreneurial backgrounds, including young professionals, innovators, entrepreneurs, researchers and academics.





**What Fellows Will Gain**

Selected fellows will have access to: 

1. Mentorship and networking opportunities
2. Capacity-building and training programmes
3. Acceleration and incubation support
4. A community of like-minded young innovators and changemakers
5. Opportunities to connect with experts, entrepreneurs and sustainability practitioners
6. Support to refine and develop innovative ideas
7. An opportunity to progress to the next phase focused on implementing developed innovative ideas and solutions



**Fellowship Focus**

The fellowship supports innovative solutions addressing one or more of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals, including areas such as: 

1. Climate action
2. Clean energy
3. Quality education
4. Decent work and economic growth
5. Gender equality
6. Sustainable cities
7. Environmental sustainability
8. Poverty reduction
9. Social innovation
10. Green entrepreneurship





**Application Deadline: September 24, 2026**
