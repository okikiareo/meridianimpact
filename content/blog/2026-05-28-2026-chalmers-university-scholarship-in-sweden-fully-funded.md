**The Chalmers University Scholarship** offers fully funded opportunities that cover tuition and a competitive salary for a five-year period, relocation support, and access to professional development.



The Chalmers University Scholarship Sweden is a fully funded research role within one of Europe’s leading technical universities.

The scholarships are offered as fully funded PhD positions for international students and those from Sweden to contribute to research, development, and advancement of science in several fields.

Available fields range from physics, engineering, AI, computing, maritime, communication, software, environmental and life sciences, architecture, etc.





**Eligibility Requirements for Chalmers University Scholarship Sweden 2026**

To be considered for the Chalmers University Scholarship Sweden, you must:

* have a Bachelor’s and/or a Master’s degree of 120 credits or a Master’s degree (magisterexamen) of 60 credits in of 120 credits
* Show proficiency in English (oral and written). Knowledge of Swedish is an advantage but not required at the time of application.
* Have strong research aptitude in both qualitative and quantitative methods.
* Be self-driven, analytical, and passionate about creating real-world impact through research. 

**Selected Candidates will be expected to;**

* Conduct high-quality empirical research in collaboration with industry
* Publish in high-impact journals and present at global conferences
* Engage in teaching, student mentoring, and contribute to department activities (up to 20% of your time)
* Complete the PhD within 4 to 5 years.



**Benefits of Chalmers University Scholarship Sweden**

* Full-time, temporary position (up to 5 years)
* Competitive salary of of 34,550 SEK ($3,697.14) per month and social security benefits
* Relocation support and access to professional development
* Inclusive, gender-balanced, and internationally friendly academic culture.

**Joining this program offers more than a PhD:**

* Collaborate with global industry leaders and academics
* Get access to top-tier labs like the Stena Industry Innovation Lab
* Engage in national and international research networks
* Be mentored by a vibrant, interdisciplinary team under the Maintenance Engineering Group
* Receive a full salary and employee benefits during the 4- to 5-year program.





 

**How to Apply for Chalmers University Scholarship 2026 (Step by Step)**

To apply for the Chalmers University Scholarship Sweden 2026, interested and qualified candidates should;

* Please apply for a position by choosing the position in the list, 
* Supporting materials, such as your CV, motivation letter, academic transcripts, and any relevant research experience, will be required.
* Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so early submission is strongly encouraged.
