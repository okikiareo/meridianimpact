UBC Sauder School of Business is giving merit scholarships to top MBA applicants for the 2027 intake.

This isn’t need-based. It’s for people with strong grades, leadership, and community impact.

If you get in, you could get between $20,000 and $60,000 CAD off your MBA. For Canadian citizens/PRs, there’s even a chance at full tuition. 

UBC is one of Canada’s top schools and Sauder grads plug straight into finance, tech, and consulting networks in Vancouver + globally. 7e4f



**Who Can Apply**

* Hold a recognized bachelor’s degree
* Strong academic record + leadership experience + community involvement
* Proficient in English: IELTS/TOEFL if needed
* Citizenship: Open to Nigerians. International students, Canadian citizens, and permanent residents all eligible 



**Eligible Countries**

All countries - Canadian, Permanent Residents, and International Students 



**Benefits**

* International students: $20,000 – $60,000 CAD deducted from tuition
* Canadian/PR students: Up to full tuition coverage
* Access to UBC Sauder’s business network, career support, and global alumni
* Recognition as a top incoming MBA candidate 



> *Canada is one of the most accessible places for Nigerians to get a world-class MBA with funding. UBC Sauder actively admits international students and the scholarship is merit-based, so your grades + leadership story matter more than your bank account.*
>
> *Vancouver also has a growing Nigerian/Canadian business community, and Sauder’s MBA is known for placing grads in North American and European firms.* 



**Deadline**

* January 8, 2027 for international applicants
* May 4, 2027 for Canadian citizens and PRs
* Document deadline: June 30, 2027 



**How to Apply**

1. Apply for admission to the UBC Sauder MBA 2027 program first

2. Submit all required documents before the deadline - you’ll be automatically considered for the scholarship
