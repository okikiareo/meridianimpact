The Australian Government supports international students with full funding via the Charles Darwin University RTP Scholarship to study in Australia.

The Charles Darwin University RTP Scholarship guarantees full tuition coverage and a $35,489 stipend per year. Funding is valid for three years full-time for a doctoral degree and two years for a research Master’s degree. 

The Charles Darwin University (CDU) Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship round is now open, offering a fantastic opportunity for both domestic and international students to pursue a Higher Degree by Research (HDR) in a vibrant and supportive environment.



The Charles Darwin University RTP Scholarship offers prospective Master’s and PhD students the opportunity to take their research potentials to the next level in a top Australian university.

All Scholarships are considered and awarded through a competitive process. Interested applicants should read this post in full for a breakdown of the application procedure.







**Eligibility Requirements for Charles Darwin University Scholarship 2026**

To be considered for the Charles Darwin University Scholarship, you must:

* must be enrolled in an accredited HDR course of study at an Australian Higher Education Provider (HEP)
* demonstrate exceptional research potential to undertake an HDR.
* must not receive income from another source to support your general living costs while enrolled in a course of study if that income is greater than 75% of the RTP stipend rate.





**Benefits of Charles Darwin University RTP Scholarship 2026**

* Australian Government RTP Stipend Scholarships are awarded to students of exceptional research potential to undertake an HDR at an Australian university.
* The scholarships are provided to assist with general living costs for three years full-time for a doctoral degree and two years for a research Master’s degree.
* AU$35,489 (2026 rate) per annum tax-free for full-time students, paid in fortnightly instalments.
* AU$17,744.50 (2026 rate) per annum, taxable, for part-time students, paid in fortnightly instalments.

**Additional benefits include:**

* paid sick leave
* maternity leave
* relocation allowance. The relocation allowance for RTP stipend recipients is up to $2,000 for domestic students and $2,500 for international students. 

 



**To apply for the Charles Darwin University RTP Scholarship, interested and qualified applicants should;**

* Visit the scholarship webpage
* Apply for a Master’s by Research or a Doctoral degree at CDU
* Apply for the scholarship using the APPLY button on the scholarship information page
*  Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarships will be by invitation only. Your application will be considered and you will be invited for the scholarship.
