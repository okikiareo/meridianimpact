**Who can Apply?**

* Product-based startups worldwide
* Early-stage and growth-stage startups
* Businesses developing innovative consumer products
* Technology-enabled product startups
* Founders seeking investment, product validation, and global market expansio



***The competition welcomes innovative solutions across sectors including:*** 

* *Business directory listing*
* Consumer Products
* Technology
* Product Development
* Manufacturing
* E-commerce
* Entrepreneurship
* Business Innovation



**Funding**

Selected startups will compete for:

* USD 50,000–200,000 in funding
* Investor exposure and funding opportunities
* Strategic partnerships
* Mentorship from industry experts
* Global visibility and product showcase opportunities
* Access to Alibaba’s innovation and commercial ecosystem

Participants will have the opportunity to:

* Access funding to accelerate product development
* Connect with global investors and industry leaders
* Build strategic business partnerships
* Receive mentorship from experienced entrepreneurs and experts
* Validate products for international markets
* Increase global visibility and commercial opportunities
* Scale their businesses through Alibaba’s international ecosystem



**Expected Outcomes**

The programme aims to support startups in:

* Commercialising innovative products
* Strengthening investment readiness
* Expanding into global markets
* Building sustainable business growth
* Forming long-term strategic partnerships
