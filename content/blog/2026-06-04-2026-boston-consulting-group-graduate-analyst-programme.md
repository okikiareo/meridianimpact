The Boston Consulting Group Graduate Analyst Programme is ideal for outstanding graduates and early career starters who are interested in discovering a career path within strategy consulting.

A GAP year at BCG will provide you with on-the-job exposure and best-in-class training from the BCG network.



**Are you a recent graduate? The BCG Graduate Analyst Programme (GAP) is a great launching pad! And the organization is actively hiring consultants from a wide range of industries and backgrounds.** 



BCG has a proud heritage of working in Nigeria, dating back to 2016. Today, we have the most significant footprint of any premium consulting firm and continue to experience substantial growth.

While each office retains its distinctive character, the system as a whole is a popular destination because of its can-do, laid-back culture. We house Consultants, Project Leaders, Product Managers, Engineers, Designers, Marketers, and more.





**2026 BCG Graduate Analyst Programme Requirements:**

Each application should meet the following requirements:

1. Excellent academic results from an accredited Nigerian university i.e. second class upper and above.
2. Completed bachelor degree within two and a half years of application. Preferably completed the NYSC or about to get called up for service.
3. In addition to academic records, we are looking for extensive extra-curricular activities and awards to demonstrate evidence of your impact.





**Benefits of Boston Consulting Group Graduate Analyst Program**

BCG offers a comprehensive benefits program, including ;

1. medical, dental and vision coverage,
2. telemedicine services,
3. life, accident and disability insurance,
4. parental leave and family planning benefits,
5. caregiving resources, mental health offerings,
6. a generous retirement program,
7. financial guidance, paid time off, and more.

**For Associates**

As an associate, you’ll work on a case team with talented, supportive BCGers who will help you learn and challenge you to grow. You’ll be responsible for specific components of a project, such as analyzing a client’s performance or conducting interviews with key market players or industry specialists.





To join our team as an associate or senior associate, you need to have the following: 

1. Excellent academic results from a recognized university i.e. second class upper and above.
2. Preferably completed, or completing a masters or PhD degree.
3. We equally value candidates with an impressive record of relevant working experience. For associates, we require 3+ years of working experience and 5+ years for senior associates.

 

**How To Apply for Boston Consulting Group Graduate Analyst Program 2026**

To apply successfully for the Boston Consulting Group Graduate Analyst Program, interested and qualified applicants should apply using the link below. 

During the online application, you will need to submit the following:

1. Your resume.
2. High school transcripts i.e. Ordinary or Advanced Level certificates.
3. All university transcripts.
