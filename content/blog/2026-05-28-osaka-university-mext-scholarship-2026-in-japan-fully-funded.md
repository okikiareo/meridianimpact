**Osaka University MEXT Scholarship 2026** for International Students

Applications are invited to apply for the **Osaka University MEXT** Scholarship 2026 in Japan. All international students are eligible to apply for the **Japanese Government (MEXT**) Scholarships. Candidates who want to study in Japan on a fully funded scholarship are urged to apply for this Osaka University scholarship. The Osaka University Scholarships are available in all academic fields/majors to study an Undergraduate Degree, Master’s Degree Program or PhD Degree Program. Also, apply for Embassy of Japan Scholarships 2026 | Study Free in Japan.



International students can study fully financed undergraduate, graduate, and doctorate degrees in Japan under the MEXT Scholarships. Awarded by Japan’s Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science, and Technology (MEXT), this coveted scholarship covers tuition fees, a monthly stipend, and travel expenses. Osaka University offers a diverse learning environment with a wide range of degrees in the arts, social sciences, engineering, and science. Important documentation such as academic transcripts, a research proposal, and recommendation letters must be submitted by qualified applicants. The MEXT Scholarship from Osaka University improves students’ experiences by providing access to cutting-edge research facilities and a thriving global community.



Osaka University is a national research university established in 1931 in Japan. The University offers international students a path to academic and professional success by covering tuition, a monthly stipend, and round-trip airfare, allowing them to concentrate on their studies. With a dedication to developing global leaders, Osaka University is a great option for students who want to further their education while experiencing Japan’s rich culture and state-of-the-art research environment. In the 2025 QS World University Rankings by Quacquarelli Symonds, Osaka University was ranked 80th globally and third in Japan, and in the 2025 Times Higher Education World University Rankings, it was ranked 175th globally. 



**Financial Benefits:**

Osaka University MEXT Scholarship 2026 are available to international students worldwide. These scholarships will cover the following:



* The full tuition fee will be given
* Monthly stipend for living expenses.
* Funded round-trip airfare.
* Health insurance
* Access to advanced research facilities.
* Chances to experience Japanese culture.
* Documents Required:
* Completed application form
* Academic transcripts
* Degree certificates
* Research proposal or study plan
* Language proficiency proof
* Valid passport copy
* Medical certificate
* CV or Resume
* Abstracts of theses (*Only if applicable)
* Letter of Recommendations.
* IELTS Score Report
* Letter of Motivation



**Eligibility Criteria:**

Applicants have to meet the given below criteria to apply for Osaka University MEXT Scholarship:



* Students whose country has a diplomatic relation with Japan can apply.
* At the time of enrollment, a high school diploma is required for undergraduates, a bachelor’s degree for master’s, or a master’s degree for PhD candidates.
* Excellent research potential and a strong academic record are necessary.
* Depending on the curriculum, proficiency in either Japanese or English is required.
* For the majority of programs, applicants must have been born on or after April 2, 1990.
* Candidates must fulfill the health standards set forth by the Japanese government.





**How to Apply for Osaka University MEXT Scholarship?**

Verify the eligibility requirements: Make sure you fulfill the prerequisites for eligibility.

* Get the necessary paperwork ready: Collect the required paperwork, such as your degree certificates, research proposal, recommendation letters, academic transcripts, and evidence of your language skills (either Japanese or English).
* Apply to the Embassy or Consulate of Japan: During the specified application period, which is usually November to January, submit your completed application to the Japanese Embassy or Consulate in your country of residence.
* Go through the initial screening: Participate in the initial screening, which consists of an interview, a written test, and a documentary review.
* Await the decision from MEXT: In consultation with Osaka University, MEXT will assess your application if it passes the initial screening. By February of the following year, you will get a notification of the scholarship award.
