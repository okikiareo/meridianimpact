The programme offers hands-on training in **AI engineering and AI application development**, helping participants build industry-relevant skills, work on real-world projects, and prepare for careers in the rapidly growing AI ecosystem.



 



**Fellowship Tracks**

Applicants can choose one of two learning tracks based on their interests and career goals:

1. **AI Engineering Track**

Participants will learn:

* Machine Learning and Model Fine-tuning
* MLOps and AI Infrastructure
* Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Systems
* Cloud Deployment using tools such as SageMaker and Docker
* AI Security and Model Optimization

2. **AI Application Track**

Participants will learn:

* Next.js and modern web frameworks
* AI API integration (OpenAI, Claude, and others)
* React and component-based development
* Product Design and User Experience (UX) for AI tools
* Backend integration and database management



**Programme Benefits**

Successful applicants will receive: Nigerian employment opportunities

* ₦50,000 monthly stipend
* Expert mentorship from AI professionals
* Hands-on experience through real-world AI projects
* Job placement and career support
* Certificate of completion
* Access to premium learning materials, software, and tools
* Opportunity to build a strong AI portfolio



**Programme Locations**

Training will take place at:

1. ICT Park, Abeokuta
2. ICT Park, Kano



**Programme Timeline**

1. Applications Open: 20 July 2026
2. Application Deadline: 31 July 2026
3. Selection Process: 3–15 August 2026
4. Training Begins: 1 September 2026



**How to Apply**

Interested applicants should submit their applications before the deadline through the Bosun Tijani Foundation website.

**Apply here**: https://www.bosuntijanifoundation.org/
