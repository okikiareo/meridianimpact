The European Union LASOMA Scholarships offer fully sponsored Masters and PhD degrees that covers tuition fees, health insurance, research workshops, and a living stipend of €14,760 per year.


**European Union LASOMA Scholarship Details:**

The LASOMA Scholarship (Lasers for Net-Zero Solar Systems Manufacture) is an Intra-Africa Academic Mobility Scheme funded by the European Union in partnership with the African Union.
LASOMA is a flagship African scholarship and research initiative building capacity for sustainable solar systems component manufacturing through laser-based manufacturing integrated with AI, machine learning, and big data science. The program is offered in partnership with six institutions from four countries for a fully funded 2-year Masters or a 3-year PhD degree program.


**Eligibility Requirements for LASOMA Masters and PhD Scholarships 2026To be considered for the LASOMA Masters and PhD Scholarships, you must:**

Possess a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a relevant Engineering discipline.

* Demonstrate strong academic and research potential
* Meet the specific academic and language requirements of the host university.
* Not have previously received scholarship under any Intra-Africa Academic Mobility Scheme 2022-2027 project 
* Project targets: 30% women, 10% special needs.
* Applicants from under-represented groups strongly encouraged.
* Research interests must align with LASOMA focus areas (laser manufacturing, solar systems, climate change).  
* Be national and resident of an African country at time of application.



**LASOMA Scholarships offers;**

* 100% tuition fees covered by European Commission funding
* Monthly stipend for living expenses throughout the programme€1230 for PhD€890 for Masters
* Medical insurance coverage
* Research consumables and equipment access at partner universities
* Travel support for preparatory school, research visits, and conferences
* Access to industry partners (BStars, DWA) and laser manufacturing facilities
* LASOMA Preparatory Graduate School included (soft skills + technical training)
* Required Documents for LASOMA Intra-Africa Academic Mobility Scheme



**To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;**
Application form, CV, Academic Transcripts, Certificates, Research Proposal, Two recommendation forms, 2-minute elevator pitch video ( for PhD Applicants only)
