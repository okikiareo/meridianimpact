The Wema Bank ALAT Campus Ambassadors Program offers financial rewards, as well as access to training, internship, and networking opportunities.





**Wema Bank ALAT Campus Ambassador Program Details:**

ALAT by Wema has opened applications for its Campus Ambassador Program for students across Nigerian campuses.

The ALAT Campus Ambassador programme is designed for students who are active on campus, good with social media, and interested in digital banking, networking, and leadership opportunities.



ALAT Ambassadors Program is a student-led community on Nigerian campuses that helps students learn about digital banking and smarter money habits.



**Eligibility Requirements for Wema Bank Campus Ambassadors Program**

To be considered for the Wema Bank Campus Ambassadors Program, you must:



* Be a student in a Nigerian tertiary institution
* Be at least 18 years old
* Have strong campus presence or influence
* Be active on social media
* Have leadership and communication skills
* Own a phone number and email address



**Benefits of Wema Bank ALAT Campus Ambassadors Program 2026**

Benefits of the ALAT Campus Ambassador Program include;



* Financial rewards
* Internship opportunities with ALAT
* Access to training opportunities
* Networking opportunities with Wema Bank leadership
* VIP access to selected events and activities
* Required Documents for Wema Bank Campus Ambassadors Program

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following details and documents;



* First name and last name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Date of birth
* School details
* Information about their reach and influence
* Motivation for joining the programme



**How to Apply for Wema Bank ALAT Campus Ambassadors Program 2026 (Step by Step)**

To apply for the Wema Bank ALAT Campus Ambassadors Program, interested and qualified candidates should;



* Complete the application form and attach all require documents.
* Explain your influence and motivation
* You may be asked about your social media presence, leadership experience, and why you want to become an ambassador.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.



**Deadline: Not Specified**
