Want to get a degree in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, or South Sudan without paying tuition?

The EAC Student Mobility Scholarship by the Inter-University Council for East Africa is doing exactly that.

**For 2026/2027, they’re funding 197 students to study at 28 partner universities in a different EAC country from their own.**

Goal: build regional talent and get you exposed to East African schools, culture, and networks. 



**Who Can Apply**

* Open to nationals and residents of EAC Partner States. You can apply to study in a country that is not your own.
* Levels: Diploma, Undergraduate, Master’s, PhD
* Age limits: Under 22 for Undergraduate. Under 35 for Master’s. Under 45 for PhD
* Academic: Meet the host university’s entry requirements. Master’s/PhD need Upper Second Class or Distinction 



Note: Nigeria is not an EAC member state. This is for Nigerians with dual citizenship or residency in an EAC country: Burundi, Kenya, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda.



**Eligible Countries**

Host Countries: Burundi, Kenya, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda

Applicants: Citizens/residents of any EAC Partner State 



**Benefits**

This is a cost-sharing scholarship:

* Host University: Waives tuition and examination fees
* IUCEA: Covers travel, health insurance, and research lump sum for Master’s & PhD
* You/Guardian: Handle accommodation, daily living, and other university fees
* Application Fee: None



> *Even though Nigeria isn’t in the EAC, many Nigerians have family ties, dual citizenship, or residency in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, etc. If that’s you, this is a legit way to get a fully supported degree abroad without the Europe/US price tag.*
>
> *Plus, studying in East Africa gives you regional exposure + cheaper cost of living than the West. IUCEA has been expanding the program with a EUR 9 million grant from Germany, so slots are increasing.* 



**Deadline: 20 August 2026**
