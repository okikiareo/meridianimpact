The **Meridian Impact Foundation's Wildcard Sponsorship** Program is designed to fund bold, unconventional student-led ideas and projects.  

We are calling on creators and thinkers who refuse to follow the norm; the ones who see opportunities where others see obstacles.  

If you have an idea you’ve been sitting on because it feels “too different” or “too risky,” this is your platform to think freely, dream big, and bring it to life with full sponsorship support.



**Eligible Country** 

* Open to applicants in Nigeria.  
* Students all around Nigeria are eligible to apply, regardless of location or institution.



**Who Can Apply**

We are looking for:  

* Students at undergraduate, graduate, or doctoral level in any field of study
* Daring creators, innovators, and thinkers with unconventional project ideas
* Individuals who challenge existing norms and propose creative solutions to real problems
* Applicants who can demonstrate passion, clarity of vision, and the potential for impact
* No GPA requirement. We care more about the idea and the person behind it than traditional academic metrics





**Sponsorship Benefits**

* Full or partial project funding to bring your idea to life
* Mentorship and resources from the Meridian Impact Foundation network
* Platform and visibility to showcase your work to a global audience





**Application Deadline**

**Rolling applications - but early submission is encouraged.**
