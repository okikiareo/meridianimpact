**Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarship**s are prestigious international opportunities, designed and funded by the European Union, and delivered by a group of higher education institutions (HEI).

They involve at least three HEI from three different countries, of which at least two must be EU Member States and third countries associated to the Programme (previously called Programme Countries).

By supporting these jointly recognised Master degrees, the EU aims at fostering excellence and internationalisation of the participating institutions.

There are also scholarships for students to take part in these prestigious programmes; the masters themselves offer them to the best-ranked applicants worldwide.



**Requirements for 2026 Erasmus Mundus Scholarship**

To be considered for the Erasmus Mundus Scholarship, you must:

* Have a bachelor’s degree (first degree) or be in your last year of bachelor studies and graduate before the master’s programme starts
* Possess an academic transcript of records 
* Satisfy the requirements of the program you are applying for.
* Meet the English Language requirements of the program.



**Benefits of Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarship**

Erasmus Mundus Scholarships are offered to the best-ranked students worldwide. The scholarships cover your participation costs and contribute to

* travel
* visa
* a living allowance
* Additionally,
* The Scholarship period will run for 1 to 2 academic years
* The Scholarships cover the cost of a student’s participation in the programme, travel and a living allowance
* The scholarship offers €1400 Monthly Stipend for selected scholars.



**Required Documents for 2026 Erasmus Mundus Scholarship**

To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents;

* Application form (to be completed online)
* Copy of Passport/National ID
* Statement of financial assets
* Research Plan
* Study Plan
* Recommendation letter
* IELTS Certificate or any other English Proficiency test





**How to Apply for Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarship**

To apply for the Erasmus Mundus Masters Scholarship, interested and qualified applicants should follow the procedures below;

* Visit the European Union scholarship webpage
* Select your desired programme from the Programme Catalogue
* Check the requirements of each program
* Apply and upload the required documents.

**If your application is successful, you will be screen and shortlisted for interview (depends on your program).**



 

**Deadline: Varies by Program**
