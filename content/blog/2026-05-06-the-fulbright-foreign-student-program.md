Fulbright Foreign Student Program 2027–2028: Fully Funded Master’s Scholarships in the United States

The Fulbright Foreign Student Program has announced its 2027–2028 application cycle, offering fully funded scholarships for talented professionals and graduates from Bangladesh to pursue master’s degrees in the United States.

The program is sponsored by the U.S. Department of State and administered by the Institute of International  Education (IIE), making it one of the most prestigious international exchange opportunities available. 

The application call, issued through the U.S. Embassy in Bangladesh, highlights a strong interest in candidates who are committed to academic excellence, leadership development, and strengthening U.S.–Bangladesh relations through  education and professional engagement.



**About the Fulbright Foreign Student Program**

The Fulbright Foreign Student Program provides scholarships for one to two years of graduate study in the United States, starting in September 2027 for selected candidates.

The program is designed to support emerging leaders, particularly junior faculty members, researchers, and professionals working in academia, think tanks, NGOs, and both public and private sector institutions.

Participants are expected to return to their home countries after completing their studies and apply their knowledge to advance national development and international cooperation.

The program covers a wide range of academic disciplines, reflecting its multidisciplinary approach to global challenges. Applicants may apply in the following fields:

Artificial Intelligence and Computer  Science

Materials Science and Technology Policy

Finance and International Business

**4. Social Sciences and Policy**

Energy and Natural Resources Management

Teaching English as a Second Language (TESOL)

**Eligibility Requirements**

Applicants to the Fulbright Foreign Student Program must meet several key criteria: Science

Hold at least a four-year bachelor’s degree with strong academic performance

Be a citizen and resident of Bangladesh at the time of application

Have at least two years of full-time professional experience relevant to their field

Demonstrate English proficiency (minimum TOEFL score of 90 or IELTS score of 7)

Not currently hold a U.S. degree or be enrolled in a U.S. academic program

Not hold a master’s degree from a foreign country (except Bangladesh)

Meet additional eligibility rules, including visa-related requirements (such as not being subject to 212(e))

Preference is given to candidates with a strong academic background and a clear plan for applying their U.S. education to contribute to development and international collaboration. 





**Application Requirements**

Applicants must submit a complete online application through the official portal, along with supporting documents, including: EducationalResources

Academic transcripts and certificates (bachelor’s and master’s, if applicable)

Three recommendation letters submitted directly by referees

TOEFL or IELTS test scores

GRE or GMAT scores (if available)

Academic records information form

All materials must be submitted before the deadline, and incomplete applications may be rejected.



**Important Deadline**

**The application deadline for the 2027–2028 cycle is:**

**July 11, 2026 (11:59 p.m. Bangladesh Standard Time)**

**Late submissions are not accepted under any circumstances.**



**Program Benefits**

Selected candidates under the Fulbright Foreign Student Program receive comprehensive financial support, which typically includes:

* Full tuition coverage
* Living stipend for the duration of study
* Health insurance support
* Travel expenses to and from the United States
* Academic and professional development opportunities

This funding structure allows scholars to focus fully on their studies and research without financial burden.



**How Applications Are Submitted**

Applications must be completed online through the official Fulbright application portal provided by the U.S. Embassy. Candidates are strongly advised to prepare early, ensuring that recommendation letters and test scores are submitted on time.

For inquiries, applicants may contact the embassy’s exchange program office directly via email.

The 2027–2028 cycle of the Fulbright Foreign Student Program represents a significant opportunity for Bangladeshi students and professionals seeking advanced study in the United States. Administered by the U.S. Embassy in Bangladesh, the program continues to support global academic exchange, leadership development, and cross-cultural understanding.

For qualified candidates, this scholarship offers not only access to world-class education but also a platform to contribute meaningfully to national and international development upon return home.
