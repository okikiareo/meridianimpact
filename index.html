About MIF

The Wildcard Sponsorship Program is designed to help students take initiative, lead themselves, and bring bold ideas to life.

MIF is committed to fostering environmental leadership, stewardship, innovation, and youth empowerment. Through funding and collaboration, we strive to create a resilient future; by nurturing young leaders, we pave the way for a thriving, interconnected planet.

Citizenship Science

Meridian Impact Foundation Citizenship Science Program empowers students to use science, creativity, and leadership to solve real community challenges. Through hands-on research, innovation, and action, young leaders turn bold ideas into sustainable solutions that support healthier communities and a thriving planet.
