About PIC

The Policy Innovation Centre (PIC) is the first institutionalized behavioral initiative in Nigeria.

We support government and stakeholders in making behaviorally informed decisions and generate evidence for impact driven interventions in critical thematic areas. The PIC is an initiative of the Nigeria Economic Summit Group (NESG), a leading think tank in Africa redefining evidence based policy advocacy.

Behavioural Insights

The Policy Innovation Centre (PIC) applies design thinking and evidence-based approaches to inform policy design and implementation across sectors. Through cutting-edge policy research and advocacy, we drive and influence conversations to catalyze sustainable development goals and social impact in sub-Saharan Africa. Using groundbreaking research and evaluations, we support governments, and policymakers to interpret and apply evidence for social impacts in Sub-Saharan Africa.
